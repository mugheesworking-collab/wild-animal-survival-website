## 1. Executive overview

Wild Animal Survival Sim 3D is an Android, third-person, open-world animal survival simulation game that blends exploration, predator/prey encounters, hunting, basic survival needs, growth/progression, animal customization, and developing family/den systems. Players select a wild animal and try to survive in expansive wilderness landscapes. They look for prey, attack, eat, drink, rest and recover, avoid or fight dangerous wildlife, and become stronger through growth stages. The project is designed for mobile devices and should be represented with believable in-game visuals rather than misleading AAA-console imagery.

### Central player fantasy

**Live as a wild animal. Explore the wilderness. Hunt to survive. Grow stronger. Build and protect your animal family.**

### Suggested concise site positioning (review before publishing)

> Step into the wild in Wild Animal Survival Sim 3D. Explore open-world environments, play as different wild animals, hunt for food, manage survival needs, grow from a young animal to a powerful Prime-stage survivor, and experience family and den gameplay.

This should be checked against the currently released version before being used as factual public copy.

## 3. Core survival gameplay [OWNER-DESCRIBED CORE]

The basic player loop is:

1. Select a playable animal.
2. Enter an open-world environment.
3. Explore, locate animals and water sources, hunt and interact with wildlife.
4. Monitor the survival state: health, stamina, hunger, thirst, rest/sleep.
5. Eat after hunting; drink at a water source; sleep/rest to recover.
6. Escape, resist or fight predators and enemies.
7. Earn progress, grow and unlock additional experiences or cosmetics.
8. With the evolving family system, interact with the den and care for the animal family.

### Mechanics mentioned by the owner

- Third-person animal movement, exploration and camera follow/zoom.
- Hunting prey with animal attack interactions.
- Vital resource management: health, stamina, hunger, thirst and sleep/rest.
- Animal-to-animal combat and survival against hostile wildlife.
- Dynamic day/night atmosphere and weather effects (see environment section).
- Growth stages with a Prime end stage.
- Animal and map selection.

**Website treatment:** Visual gameplay-focused sections with strong headlines, small explanatory paragraphs, and real media. Don't guarantee advanced mechanics that have only been proposed.
